## Surface

Mode: Persuade. Audience: car enthusiasts who like collecting. Action: join the waitlist (email form, working in the first viewport). Build path: code-led (the image connector only returns thumbnail-size images, too small to comp a full page).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kuruma looks and feels like a modern collectible card game in the Pokémon TCG Pocket mould, so a car fan understands within seconds that this is a card collection, not a racing game. The cards are the heroes. It refuses the dark, neon-glow trading-card template.

OWN-WORLD: Cool off-white ground (#f5f6fa) with white surfaces, deep navy ink, and one racing-red accent kept for actions. Cards are rounded white frames with a photo window, the name in Figtree extra-bold (Unbounded is too wide for small cards), rarity shown as 1–4 diamonds (slate, blue, violet, gold), and a rainbow holo foil that follows the pointer. Soft, low-saturation tier-colour fields sit behind the hero. Unbounded for display, Figtree for body. Shadows have offset and blur.

STORY: The visitor sees a floating fan of car cards, reads "every car culture, one collection", and can leave an email right away. Below, they open a demo booster pack and see five cards flip out with their rarities. Then three pillars (collect, trade, build your garage), each shown with real card arrangements. A final call to join the waitlist closes the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred headline in Unbounded at the top, subline beneath, then a pill-shaped email field with a red submit button. Below it, a five-card fan with the legendary card in the middle, floating with restrained depth parallax on scroll and pointer. Nav: wordmark on the left, dark (ink) waitlist button on the right, so the hero submit stays the only red action.

FORM: canon (the genre standard played straight; the user took the standing exit). Reference: Pokémon TCG Pocket. Seed key ef863f24. Signature interaction: the booster pack opens and its cards flip out. Motion grammar: spring-based, exponential ease-out, parallax capped at a small amplitude, and all of it disabled under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
