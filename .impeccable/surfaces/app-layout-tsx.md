## Surface

Mode: Operate for the game screens (boosters, collection, album, shop, trades, achievements, profiles), Persuade for the landing (`app/page.tsx`) and `/connexion`. Audience: car enthusiasts who collect. Action: sign in with Discord, then open packs every session. Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine). The redesign replaces the light "SaaS" world across every route and gives all nine boosters one shared art direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kuruma is a mobile-standard collectible card game, played straight at the craft level of Pokémon TCG Pocket and Marvel Snap: the player always knows it is a game, never a dashboard. It refuses the airy white SaaS layout it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Deep saturated indigo ground with soft violet light pools and a faint diamond lattice; raised indigo panels with a lit top edge; chunky 3D buttons (gold primary, blue secondary, green claim) with a darker bottom edge that presses down; Russo One display set white with a dark drop, Nunito for everything read; jetons and packs as HUD pills; cards stay white physical objects with tier frames and holo on legendaries.

STORY: A visitor sees a floating fan of real cars, the shelf of nine boosters and a big Discord button; inside, a HUD shows packs and jetons, a raised Boosters button anchors the tab bar, and every reward is a big claimable moment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: headline in white Russo One centred, gold second line, Discord 3D button beneath, fact chips, the card fan rising out of a violet glow. Game: HUD top bar (wordmark, tabs, jetons, packs, bell, avatar), page title with drop, the screen's main action as the gold button.

FORM: canon (the user took the standing exit on roll 84949d1d); references Pokémon TCG Pocket and Marvel Snap. Signature interaction: the booster tear and card reveal, now on the shared pack art. Motion grammar: springs and exponential ease-out, button press depth, reduced motion respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
